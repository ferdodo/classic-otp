About

Classic OTP is an independent one-time password service.

OTP authentication is a major risk vector.

Use OTP only as a complement to another authentication method.

This service has not undergone a security audit. Do not use it in production or for sensitive or critical information.

This software is provided as-is, without warranties or guarantees.

Your email address is used only to send the OTP code. OTP codes and pending emails are held in memory during processing and deleted after validation or expiry. Nothing is written to a database.
